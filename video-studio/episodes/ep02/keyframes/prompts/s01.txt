Shot 1, start frame. Vertical 9:16, high wide view from the last rice terrace above the river, looking down to the fig village in the middle distance (the same fig, broken bridge and winding river as the plate, seen from higher and further back). A loose, straggling group of six Mosskin carriers comes down the final terrace steps toward the river bank, Tac near the back, small in frame, carrying a jar too. The carriers are NOT identical and NOT in a neat line: different sizes (one tiny young one, one big old broad one), different amounts of moss (one almost bare jade, one so mossy it has a small fern crown, one with a lichen patch), one darker basalt-grey Mosskin, slightly different head shapes and belt wear. They carry different loads in different ways: one jar on its head, one hugging a jar to its belly, one with two small jars hanging from a pole, one dragging a dented metal canister on a vine, one with a bundle of fern fronds. Irregular spacing and rhythm: two walking close together, one lagging behind, one stopped to shift its load, one a step off the path. Lilac hour, river mist, layered hills.
