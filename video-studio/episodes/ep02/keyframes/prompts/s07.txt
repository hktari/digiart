Vertical 9:16, medium-close shot inside the hall at the drip hollow: a fig root comes down the cracked composite wall and drips clear water into a small mossy hollow where a few old cups stand. Tac stands beside it, seen from the knees up so his crown is normal size in frame, holding a small chipped pale ceramic cup and tilting it so a single drop of tea hangs from the rim, about to fall onto a patch of bare earth that shows through the broken floor tiles at his feet. On that earth, a tiny tightly curled fern fiddlehead just breaking the soil. One drop only, no stream. Cool light, sleeping Mosskin softly visible in a wall niche behind.
