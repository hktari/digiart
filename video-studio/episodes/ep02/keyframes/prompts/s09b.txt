Shot 9b, start frame (rain into the shrine). Vertical 9:16, close shot in the first light rain at a small coiled-serpent shrine niche carved into a fig root, offerings of mangosteens, green figs and a little cup of tea on its ledge. A Mosskin sitting beside it holds its old cup, now holding a little rainwater, and tilts it toward the shrine so a thin trickle of rain is just about to pour into the shrine's small carved basin. Fine silver rain streaks, drops on the root, wet moss glistening. Content, reverent face. Soft grey-lilac light.
