Shot 8, start frame (the presence). Vertical 9:16, low wide shot from the clearing looking up past Tac at the fig canopy and the dense forest edge behind the village, lilac-hour sky. Tac stands small in the lower third, back half turned, ears up, looking up. Something enormous and unseen is passing through the treetops: a long, continuous diagonal line of foliage across the upper half of the frame is pressed down and parted, leaves bent and tilted all one way along that line like a wake, with a soft long shadow beneath it. The rest of the canopy is perfectly still. No creature is visible at all, only the trace of its passing. In the lower corner two sitting Mosskin have their heads slightly tilted up toward the line, content faces, calm. Leaves painted normally; no black shapes, no solid dark bands.
