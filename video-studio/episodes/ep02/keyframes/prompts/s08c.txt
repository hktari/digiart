Shot 8c, start frame (village reactions). Vertical 9:16, medium shot of three Mosskin sitting on flat stones and a root by the river, just after something enormous has passed over the canopy. Each reacts in a small, calm, different way, with content faces: one has turned its head and is glancing up and over its shoulder; one has its head tilted to one side, listening; one lifts its small old cup slightly toward the sky in a quiet salute. Fig roots and a serpent shrine niche behind them, slow river beside. Lilac-hour light, very still air.
