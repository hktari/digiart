## What the photos add

- **Places:** the Still Water (mirror lake, fig-strangled tree, collapsed stilt houses), the Peacock Door (sealed door under a fig).
- **Statues:** glass-marble eyes and coloured glass inlays; the glass-eyed yaksha guardian; blocky limewashed naga heads; a kala-face plinth motif.
- **Leftovers:** banner poles with tarnished silver strings, the millstone cascade, palm-sized clay discs.
- **Plants:** dumb cane, elephant ear, lady palm, bird's-nest fern, bottle palm, caladium (muted rose), green bananas, ti plants (plum).
- **Textures:** limewash streaked with rain and mildew for all old white stone; leaf-shingle roofs for Mosskin shelters; backlit translucent leaves.
- **Left out:** Buddha, deva and monk figures, temple buildings, spirit-house offerings, litter, wires, harsh noon light.
