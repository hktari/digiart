"The Sleeping Temple" - wide view of an old temple compound reclaimed by forest: long hall on a whitewashed base, three-tier roof of muted dull-red tiles patched with moss and small trees, curling naga finials, dull silver-grey gable; a strangler fig pouring roots over the back; a mildewed white bell-shaped spire with a dull gold tip and a fig in its tiers; a limewashed naga stair to the dark doorway; two banner poles with tarnished silver strings; a mossy white guardian lion. Mosskin live here: sleeping on the top step, sitting on the lion's plinth, carrying a clay disc up the stair, peering from a window. Mist, rising fireflies.
