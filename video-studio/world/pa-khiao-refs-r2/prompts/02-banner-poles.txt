"The Banner Poles" - a mossy forest clearing ringed by tall trees. Two very tall, thin poles stand in it, tarnished brass gone dull green, each topped by a small crossbar with a stylised bird finial. From each end of the crossbar hang long strings of small hammered-metal ornaments, gone soft grey and silver with tarnish: little hollow bell-shaped tubes, flat lace-like discs, small tiered shapes, all swaying slightly. Vines climb the poles. A young Mosskin has climbed halfway up one pole by its vine and is tying a string of circuit-board shards and lens discs to it, adding its own chime to the old ones. An older mossy Mosskin sits at the foot of the pole with eyes closed, listening. Ferns, fan palms and a few wild bananas around the clearing. The ornaments catch only a faint cool sheen of the dusk sky, no glow.
