(New composition; refs: 08-naga-stair-to-temple-v1 for the architecture, Naga Stair keyframe for style.) One focal point: the temple. Low angle from the bottom of a long straight naga stair looking up its centre line; serpent balustrades small and low as leading lines, heads cropped at the bottom edge. Temple hall centred and dominant in the upper third: dark doorway under a flame-edged arch, tiered dull-red tile roof under moss, naga finials, silver-grey gable, fig roots over one side, mildewed white spire behind in the last lilac light. Strongest light and contrast on the facade, foreground in soft shadow. Clay discs on the steps lead the eye up. A tiny Mosskin on the top step, one halfway up with a clay disc. Mist and fireflies at the base. Avoid a big foreground statue, figures inside, Buddha images, monks, warm lights, bright red/orange, gold shine, text.
