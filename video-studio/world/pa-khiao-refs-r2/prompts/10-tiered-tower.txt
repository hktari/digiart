"The Tiered Tower" - a tall futuristic tower of the vanished high-tech civilisation built in the northern Thai temple style, abandoned and swallowed by forest: five or six steep overlapping Lanna-style roof tiers of pale composite panels and dull-blue hex solar tiles (many cracked or missing); naga finials are composite conduits with snapped fibre cables hanging like vines; gables are dead lattice antenna panels; dark round windows; whitewashed stepped plinth with rain stains and a stair with stylised serpent rails. A colossal strangler fig grows up through the tower. Mosskin climb the cables, sit in a window, sleep on the stair. (Uses _head/_tail; no Buddha images or deity figures, no lit windows.)
