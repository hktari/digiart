"The Swallowed Wall" - looking up from low in a dense understorey at dusk. An old wall of grey concrete blocks is almost completely swallowed by plants: huge leaves of dumb cane splashed with cream speckles, giant elephant-ear leaves, fan-leaved lady palms, wavy bird's-nest ferns, a large strangler fig trunk with ferns growing on its branches. The leaves glow softly translucent where the last sky light comes through them. Under the leaves, a small Mosskin shelter: a lean-to roofed with layers of big dry curled teak leaves overlapping like shingles, mossy at the edges. A Mosskin sits under it, mostly in shadow, only its half-smiling face and clay belt catching light. A bundle of old dead palm fronds is wedged high in the fig branches like a giant nest. Small figs on the fig in muted green and dull ochre. Lilac sky in the gaps.
