"The Naga Heads" - a close, low three-quarter view of the foot of an old stone stairway climbing up into the forest (a different stair from the reference: whitewashed, not green). Its two balustrades are long stylised serpents of limewashed stone, their bodies running down the sides of the steps, ending at the bottom in two big blocky carved serpent heads with stacked jaw ridges and round inset glass eyes of muted gold that catch a tiny cool glint. The white limewash is streaked and blotched with dark grey-green rain stains and black-green moss, cracks with ferns growing out. At the top of the steps, a tall empty pointed-arch niche, completely empty inside except for a fern growing from its floor. On the steps and the serpents' paws lie dozens of small palm-sized clay discs, some filled with rainwater. One Mosskin sits on a serpent head as if it were a bench, holding a clay disc like a cup. Frangipani-like trees and ferns around. Dusk, lilac sky, misty hills behind. Only carved stone serpents, no living serpent.
