"The Millstone Cascade" - an overgrown corner of an old garden under a fig canopy. A small water cascade built long ago from stacked round grinding stones and old concrete blocks: water spills from a spout at the top into a ring-shaped stone basin, then down into the next, and the next, into a wide pond bowl at the bottom. Every stone is green with moss and algae, wet and gleaming softly. Little clay tortoise figures sit on the rims of the basins, worn and mossy, one with a small pale stone on its back. Around it: split-leaf philodendrons, bird's-nest ferns, potted plants long grown out of their cracked pots, a weathered wooden beam. A Mosskin kneels at the bottom basin, holding an old cup under the falling water. Another sits motionless on a block, mossed over so it looks like part of the cascade, one eye open. Cool dusk light and a few fireflies.
