"The Glass-Eyed Guardian" - a tall standing guardian statue alone in the forest, not part of any building: a yaksha giant of limewashed stone with a tall pointed crown, bulging round eyes inset with clear yellow-green glass marbles, fangs curving up from the corners of a wide grimace, big hoop earrings, ornate jewelled collar and belt, a large glass cabochon at the belt, a short skirt of carved scales. It holds a long three-pronged staff, now wound tightly with a climbing vine and small white orchids. The white is streaked with grey-green rain stains, moss on the shoulders and crown, a fern on the crown tip. At its base, a square plinth carved with a fanged face surrounded by scrolling tendrils, the face's eyes inset with dull dark-red glass. In the foreground, out of focus, broad striped plum and green leaves (ti plants). A small Mosskin stands on the plinth beside the guardian's foot, gently brushing moss onto its toes; its face echoes the guardian's. Dusk, layered misty hills.
