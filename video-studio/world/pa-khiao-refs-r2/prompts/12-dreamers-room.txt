Concept art for an animated series set in Pa Khiao, a peaceful forest grown over rural northern Thailand long after the last civilisation faded. The FIRST attached image is the character sheet for the Dreamers: follow it exactly for their design. The SECOND image is the world style reference (the Sleeping Town keyframe): flat painterly 2D/2.5D storybook illustration, flat colour shapes, no outlines, no black, shading with darker shades of the same colour, soft cool light, cool yellow-green fireflies.

The Dreamers (from the sheet): people who kept upgrading until the end, each as far as they chose. Round white helmet heads with a dark visor (#14171B) showing a simple pixel face (asleep, good dream); white shell (#E3E5E6) and graphite joints (#3A3F44); some mostly human with only a visor and one robot arm, some half and half, some mostly machine. Each wears its own single neon colour as thin light lines along graft seams, a flickering halo ring or visor rim: cyan #3FE8F0, magenta #FF4FB8, lime #A8FF3E or violet #9D7BFF. Never as clothing. Faint, still lit, mossed over.
Mosskin: small stone-skinned beings of worn jade, a tall pear-shaped head as big as the body, heavy relaxed lids, broad flat nose, a soft closed-mouth content half-smile, a clay belt with square notches. Calm, never sad.

SCENE: "The Dreamers' room" - vertical 9:16, eye-level medium-wide view into a large overgrown room inside a building of the Sleeping Town at lilac dusk. Strangler-fig roots come down the walls and through a cracked ceiling; a broken skylight lets in cool light shafts. Six or seven Dreamers sit where they stopped, in reclined seats and on the floor against the walls, facing nothing, cables running from the backs of their necks to a dead wall of dark screens and to each other. Their visors show sleeping or smiling pixel faces; their neon light lines are the only colour glow in the room, each figure its own colour, all faint. Their bodies are neglected: rust and dirt at the graphite joints, moss on shoulders and helmets, a fern growing through one open hand, a small bird nesting in the chest cavity of a mostly-machine Dreamer, vines climbing the legs of one whose face smiles at nothing. Three Mosskin tend them quietly: one stands on a seat arm wiping rain off a visor with a leaf, one gently pulls a vine off a Dreamer's helmet, one sets a small cup of leaf tea at a Dreamer's feet. Nobody notices the Mosskin. Puddles on the floor, rain dripping from the skylight, open floor space in the lower third. Calm, sad-but-tender, never horror.

Avoid: warm light, orange, yellow lamps, candles, fire, bright lit screens, legible text, logos, skulls, blood, gore, horror faces, people without devices, Buddha images, photoreal or 3D render style, black outlines.
