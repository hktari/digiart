"The Still Water" - a wide, perfectly still lake in an old river bend, mirror-flat, reflecting the lilac sky, the clouds and the far tree line upside down. In the foreground on the near bank, a tall old tree being slowly strangled by a fig: pale smooth fig vines spiral and braid around its trunk and drop straight to the ground like ropes. A low rail of weathered bamboo poles along the bank, mossy. A young coconut palm with one great fan of fronds stands at the water's edge. Leaf litter, ferns and wild ginger in the foreground. On the far bank, a long low row of collapsed stilt houses, their roofs sagging under thick moss and fig roots, legs standing in the water, all clearly abandoned and reclaimed by the forest. Two Mosskin sit on the bamboo rail with their backs half to us, looking at their own reflections in the water. Mist lies on the far bank.
