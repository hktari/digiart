"The Peacock Door" - a sealed old doorway half buried in the roots of a giant strangler fig. Above the door, a carved stone pediment shaped like a flame-edged arch: inside it a peacock in relief, its tail fanned wide behind it in carved feathers, small coloured glass beads (muted green, blue and dull red) set into the feathers like eye-spots, catching faint cool glints. At either side of the pediment, small carved musician figures playing flutes, worn smooth. Scrolling leaf carving around the arch. The stone is whitewashed and streaked with grey-green rain stains and moss. The wooden door below is dark, swollen, shut, with roots grown across it; a fern grows from a crack in it. Rows of small carved leaf shapes along the lintel. A Mosskin stands before the door, head tilted up at the peacock. Fireflies drift around the roots. Dusk.
